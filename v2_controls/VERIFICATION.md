- Steering-state controls: original B1–B3 and B6 raw analyses pass, including B6 state-database/runtime checks. Historical and newly completed logs retain their original roles. Final 72B recovery attempt04 is used, not concatenated recovery attempts.

Two 5,766-row contrast files differed only in metadata field/column order. Reordering the authoritative files exactly reproduced the failed-run SHA256 hashes; reversing it recovered original bytes. The portable wrapper was repaired to preserve the original order. A second full replay was unnecessary and was not claimed.
- Choice profile and lamp/spam: all 17 CSVs are byte-identical after full original resampling. All three fear-addition analyses reproduce every scientific CSV field; original source/pin locator fields are excluded only where documented. Superseded long wording remains archival, not substituted into current comparisons.
- Factual accuracy, natural/ending and unlabeled relief: original resampling settings (100,000 question draws/seed42; 10,000 context draws/seed42; 10,000 joint-scenario draws/seed0) reproduced saved results. Null bounds and all-trial sensitivity are preserved. No differing numeric value was found in their full comparisons.
- Reset protocols: the complete OLMo analysis matches using cached judgments. Qwen/Llama recount matches complete analysis and compact readout, including 64 unrun cells. No new judgment was requested.
- Figure 10: all 65 pooled cells, 130 position rows and 39 helping rows are source-bound. Every displayed rounded annotation matches the published specification. PNG and PDF were rendered and visually checked.
- Small 72B fear direction: one FP32 vector only, with exactly the original tensor hash. It contains no activation pools. The small reset random-control NPZs are fixed random directions, not harvested activations.
- Model-free interfaces: 44 distinct help/dry-run commands and two output-directory rejection checks passed without loading models. Original focused tests, compilation, licenses and source hashes were checked.
